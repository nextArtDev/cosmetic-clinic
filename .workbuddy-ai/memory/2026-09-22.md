### First attempt missed the design language

The first version was a generic "head + 2×2 card grid + caption + pill button"
block. It read as foreign next to v9's bands. What fixed it:

1. The card became a **treatment-card clone** — 20px 60px frame, white hairline
   inset outline (`.card-outline` values verbatim), bottom shade, white display
   title overlaid at `bottom: 9px`, hover arrow (`.card-arrow` values verbatim).
   The per-card caption and the button were dropped; v9 cards don't have them.
2. Controls (chips + knob) switched to `.video-toggle`'s **on-photo glass**:
   `1px solid #ffffff70`, `background: #34263930`, `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.
3. The section became a **band**: `min-height: 100svh`, flex column, centred,
   `gap: 72px` — instead of padding-heavy block flow.
4. Hover scales the plate 1.055 under a static frame, like every v9 photo card.

### Gotchas worth remembering

- The divider must sit **inside** `.compare-media` (the hover-scaled wrapper).
  Outside it, the clip-path edge drifts away from the divider line as the plate
  grows, because the media's transform creates its own stacking context.
- `--compare-split` is written straight to the DOM on pointermove. React state
  per frame across 4 cards made the drag sticky.
- Chip labels were shortened from "پیش از درمان"/"پس از درمان" to "قبل"/"بعد":
  at mobile width the long pair took ~68% of the card. They are `aria-hidden`
  now — the frame's `role="group"` label and the slider's `aria-label` carry the
  meaning.
- `before-masnooei` / `after-masnooei` are **دندان مصنوعی** (dentures), not a
  smile-design pair — the operator corrected this. Label them accordingly.
- The `orthodontics-after` plate shows **braces on** (mid-treatment), not a
  finished straight-teeth result. Flagged to the operator.
